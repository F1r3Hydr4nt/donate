Safe use checklist
  1. Check the zip's SHA-256 against SHA256SUMS.txt before copying it to the offline computer.
  2. Unzip on a computer that is disconnected from every network. A freshly booted live USB (e.g. Tails) is best.
  3. Open index.html directly from disk. The self-test must pass.
  4. Print with a USB printer. Network printers and "print to PDF" can keep copies of the page.
  5. Close the browser when done. Don't save the page or take screenshots.
  6. Before storing real value, send a small amount, restore the wallet from the printed key, and spend it back.
  7. Anyone who sees a private key or recovery phrase can take the funds. Store the paper like cash: laminated, hidden, and with a second copy somewhere safe.
